# Logo Concept

## Goal

The 栖美地 logo should become a long-term brand asset that families can trust.

It should help users feel:

- This brand is reliable.
- This brand understands family outings.
- This brand is calm and practical.
- This brand can stay relevant for many years.

## Brand Keywords

The logo must express:

- 自然
- 家庭
- 陪伴
- 信任
- 长期主义
- 克制
- 高级

## Logo Personality

The logo should feel:

- Reliable
- Warm
- Natural
- Modern
- Long-term
- Calm
- Mature

## What It Is Not

The logo must not feel like:

- 儿童乐园
- 旅游公司
- 旅行社
- 景区
- 露营品牌
- 户外装备品牌
- 亲子游乐场

## Forbidden Directions

Do not use these as direct logo concepts:

- Leaf directly forming the logo
- Map icon
- Compass
- Mountain peak
- Tent
- Cartoon child
- Sun
- Rainbow
- Complex gradient
- Complex geometry

## Creative Direction

The logo should be abstract enough to last, but clear enough to be remembered.

Prefer:

- Simple symbolic structure
- Soft but controlled geometry
- Warm sense of place
- Subtle feeling of togetherness
- Calm negative space
- Strong silhouette

Avoid:

- Literal travel symbols
- Overused outdoor icons
- Childish elements
- Trend-based visual tricks

## Decision Standard

A good Qimeide logo should still make sense if the product expands from weekend destinations to:

- App
- Mini program
- Family decision tools
- City guides
- Offline family events
- Content media

If the logo only works for one current feature, it is too narrow.
