# Image Style

Images are one of the strongest trust signals for 栖美地.

They must help users judge whether a place is suitable for a family trip.

## Direction

- 真实家庭
- 自然光
- 纪录片风格
- 人物优先
- Natural colors
- Clear environment context

## Preferred Images

- Family interaction in real outdoor settings
- Kids playing naturally
- Parents preparing or resting
- Real routes, parking, restrooms, entrances
- Places with visible scale and safety context

## Avoid

- AI感图片
- HDR
- 旅游宣传照
- Over-polished commercial photos
- Empty scenery as the main image
- Platform watermarks
- Unverified copyrighted images

## Editing

- Crop to clarify the subject.
- Keep colors realistic.
- Avoid over-saturation.
- Do not erase meaningful context.
- Do not use unrelated fallback scenery as real destination photography.

## Main Image Rule

人物优先于景色。

When people are not available, the image should still show decision-relevant context.
