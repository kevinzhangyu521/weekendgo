# Visual Language

This document defines the visual language for 栖美地 (Qimeide).

It applies to:

- Website
- Social media
- App
- Mini program
- Video covers
- Future brand materials

## Brand Keywords

- 自然
- 家庭
- 真实
- 信任
- 陪伴
- 长期主义

## Design Style

栖美地的视觉风格应该是：

- 极简
- 留白
- 高级
- 克制
- 真实

The product should feel like a trusted weekend companion for families, not a noisy travel advertisement or a backend dashboard.

## What It Should Feel Like

- Calm enough for parents to trust.
- Warm enough for families to feel invited.
- Clear enough to support fast decisions.
- Real enough to avoid marketing fatigue.
- Durable enough to work for the next ten years.

## Forbidden Directions

Do not use:

- 儿童卡通风
- 旅游宣传风
- 高饱和撞色
- 复杂渐变
- 玻璃拟态
- 赛博风
- Heavy decorative effects
- Fake luxury treatment

## Core Rule

Design should support family decisions.

Every visual choice should help users answer:

> Is this place suitable for my family this weekend?
