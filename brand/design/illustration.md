# Illustration

Illustration is secondary to real photography.

Use illustration only when real photography is not required or not helpful.

## Style

- 扁平
- 线性
- 自然
- 柔和
- Low saturation
- Simple shapes
- Rounded details

## Usage

Use illustrations for:

- Empty states
- Onboarding
- Simple explanations
- Lightweight system messages
- Non-destination UI moments

## Color

Use Brand Kit colors only:

- Primary Green
- Secondary Surface
- Accent Orange
- Text colors
- Border colors

## Do Not

- Do not use cartoon children as the main brand style.
- Do not use mascot-heavy visual systems.
- Do not use complex scenic illustrations as destination substitutes.
- Do not use AI-looking illustration packs.
- Do not mix multiple illustration styles.

## Relationship With Photography

Photography carries trust.

Illustration carries explanation.

Never use illustration to fake a real place.
